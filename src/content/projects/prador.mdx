## The problem

Prador is a car detailing business in the UK. Customers wanted to book a slot quickly, without creating an account, and the business needed to hear about every booking straight away.

## What I built

A booking site for the business, built as a freelance client project:

- **Booking in seconds.** Customers fill in their name, phone, email, service and preferred date, with no account.
- **Checked on the server.** Every booking is validated on the server before anything is sent, and a bad request gets a clear error.
- **Two emails at once.** The customer gets a confirmation and the business gets the booking, as soon as the form is submitted.
