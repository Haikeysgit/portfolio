## The problem

Football fans predict results every week anyway. GafferScore turns that into a game, with points for getting it right and leaderboards to settle the arguments.

## What I built

GafferScore is a Premier League prediction game. Players predict the result and exact score of every fixture before kickoff, earn points for accuracy, and climb weekly and overall leaderboards. As founding full stack engineer, I built the platform: the scoring logic, the leaderboards, the prediction deadlines and the frontend.

## Key decisions

- **Leaderboards are computed in the database.** Rankings come from one database function that takes the leaderboard type and the gameweek. The ranking logic lives in one place, and pages stay fast as players are added.
- **Deadlines are strict.** All predictions lock at the first kickoff of the matchweek, so nobody can wait for early results before predicting.
