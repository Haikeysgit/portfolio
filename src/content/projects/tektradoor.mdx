## The problem

Tektradoor is my trading startup. While the trading agent is rebuilt, the site's job is to explain what Tektradoor does and collect a waitlist of serious early users.

## What I built

As the solo developer, I built the waitlist site: the home page, an engine page explaining the approach in plain language, a team page, and an early-access form.

## Key decisions

- **Plain language over jargon.** The site explains what the engine does without the technical terms, and without giving away how it decides to enter a trade.
- **A waitlist, not open sign-ups.** Access is gated so early users are the ones who will stick around.

## Results

- Live at [tektradoor.com](https://tektradoor.com/).
- The trading agent is being rebuilt in public. See the Tektradoor trading agent project.
