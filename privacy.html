Privacy Policy: Job Outreach

Last updated 3 November 2026

Job Outreach is a personal tool built and used only by Imisioluwa Obaseki to prepare job-search emails. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What Google data it uses

When Imisioluwa signs in with Google, the tool asks for these permissions on Imisioluwa's own account:

  • Gmail (compose): to create email drafts. The tool never sends email; every message is reviewed and sent by hand.
  • Gmail (read-only): to check whether a drafted email was sent, replied to or bounced, and to read the sent version so later drafts can learn from edits.
  • Google Drive (files created by the app): to keep a tracking spreadsheet that the tool itself created.

How the data is stored and shared

Results are kept in that spreadsheet in Imisioluwa's own Google Drive and on Imisioluwa's own computer. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.
